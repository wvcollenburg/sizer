Every organisation (tenant) is defined by its email domain. The first person to sign up from a domain becomes its tenant admin, shown with an Admin badge. There can be more than one. {% if scale %}Super admins can change roles in the admin panel (see Users).{% else %}Ask your Scale Computing contact if the role needs to move to someone else.{% endif %}

Managing members

{{ m.ui("Organization") }} in the header lists the active members of your organisation, with their role and last sign-in.

{{ m.shot("org-modal", "Organization: your organisation's members") }}
  • {{ m.ui("Disable") }} revokes a member's access immediately, for example when they leave the company. Their projects and sizings stay.
  • You cannot disable yourself{% if scale %} or a super admin{% endif %}.
  • New colleagues do not need an invitation: they sign up with their work email and join your organisation automatically.
{% call m.note() %}A tenant admin cannot re-enable a disabled member, change roles or delete accounts. {% if scale %}Those are super-admin actions (see Users).{% else %}Ask your Scale Computing contact for those.{% endif %} Apart from managing members, a tenant admin works like any other user. They cannot edit colleagues' projects.{% endcall %}
