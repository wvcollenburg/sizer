Sharing with colleagues

Sharing inside your organisation needs no action: your colleagues see your projects, and the sizings in them, in the My Company folder on the Projects screen (see Folders), read-only. To work on a colleague's sizing, use Copy to mine.

Share codes

Every project and every sizing has a 12-digit share code, shown on the project page and after saving. Colleagues in your organisation can open either one by its code with {{ m.ui("Open by code") }} on the Projects screen. A sizing code opens that sizing inside its project. Your Scale Computing contact can use the code to look at your project or sizing when you ask for help.

Your name on proposals

Click your name (or email address) in the header to set the name that goes on your proposals. It fills Prepared by on the projects you create from then on. Existing projects keep the name they have. Change it there under {{ m.ui("Details") }}.

Your data

{{ m.ui("Privacy & data") }} in the header:

{{ m.shot("privacy-data", "My data & privacy") }}
  • Product update emails: tick or untick at any time.
  • {{ m.ui("Download my data") }}: a JSON file with your account, your projects and sizings, and your own audit trail.
  • {{ m.ui("Delete my account") }}: closes your account and signs you out. Your projects and sizings are removed. An administrator can still undo this for 90 days, after which everything is deleted permanently.

The full Privacy Policy is linked at the bottom of every page.
