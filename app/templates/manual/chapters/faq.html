My import is refused.
Upload the .xlsx exactly as Live Optics, RVTools or Nutanix Collector produced it. Files that were opened and re-saved in another tool, or exports that are incomplete (for example a Live Optics export where the datastores cover only some of the VMs), are refused with a message that says what is wrong. Run the collection again where needed.
The recommendation looks too big.
Check, in this order: the VM review (powered-off VMs, VMware artifacts and old controller VMs still included?), the vCPU:core ratio, the growth settings (10% for 5 years is 1.6 times today), the day-one caps, and the source CPU benchmark score on step 2. The Determined by line and the limiting marker show which resource drives the size.
The recommendation looks too small.
Check that the ratio is realistic for the workload (databases want close to 1:1), that no VMs were excluded by mistake, and whether local storage should be included. Remember that CPU and RAM are already sized for one node down.
What is the difference between Alternative and Part of the total?
Parts of the total add up to one estate (sites, clusters, phases). Alternatives compete for the same workload and are never added up. See Roles.
A sizing says Needs refresh.
The catalog or the sizing rules changed after it was saved. Opening the project recalculates it. If that fails, open the sizing: the reason is shown there. A DR target must be opened and saved by hand.
A sizing says Re-import.
Its file was read by an older version of the importer that has since been fixed. Import the same file again into a new sizing.
How do I rename a sizing?
You cannot rename it. Open it, choose {{ m.ui("Save sizing") }} → {{ m.ui("Save as new") }} with the new name, and delete the old one.
I cannot change a project.
It belongs to someone else: a colleague's project, or one opened by code, is read-only. Use Copy to mine on a sizing to get your own copy.
I chose Word or PowerPoint and got an error.
The editable formats are for Scale Computing staff. Use PDF document or PDF slides{% if not scale %}, or ask your Scale Computing contact{% endif %}.
My export says Expired.
Downloads are kept for 24 hours. Make the export again.
The BOM checker does not recognise my quote.
The AI agent reads it instead. If that fails or is not available, download the blank template from the upload step, copy the parts into it, and check that file.
I did not get the verification or password-reset email.
Check your spam folder. Verification links last 24 hours and reset links 2 hours. Request a new one from the sign-in dialog. If nothing arrives, {% if scale %}check the Email settings{% else %}ask your tenant admin or your Scale Computing contact{% endif %}.
Who can see my work?
Everyone in your organisation (same email domain) can see your projects and sizings, read-only. Scale Computing staff can open a project or sizing when they have its share code. Nobody else can.
